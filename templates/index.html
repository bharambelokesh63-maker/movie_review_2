How it works
1

You provide the text

Enter a single review here, or analyze many at once on the Batch page with CSV support.

2

Python processes it

Flask routes the text through tokenization, stop-word removal, VADER scoring, TF-IDF, NER and emotion-cue detection.

3

You read the results

Sentiment label and score, opinion words, emotions, named entities, keywords and aspect-level opinions are all shown on one page.

Scope: predictions are estimates and can be wrong — sarcasm and mixed opinions are difficult for rule-based methods. Statistics describe only the reviews you supply, not all viewers of a film.
